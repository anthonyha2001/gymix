## Boost settings (recommended; the founder sets the budget)
- **Goal:** "More website visits" to `[WEBSITE]/demo` (the demo converts best; the trial comes after).
- **Audience:** Lebanon (Beirut and Mount Lebanon first), ages 22–55, interests: gym, fitness centre, personal
  trainer, CrossFit, small business owner. Use "Advantage+ audience" if offered.
- **Budget and duration:** `[AD_BUDGET]`, 7 days to start; keep the daily amount small and watch "cost per demo
  started" before raising it.
- **Placements:** Instagram Reels, Stories and Feed (+ Facebook Reels).
- **Tracking:** add `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=intro_ad` to the link.
- **Before boosting:** fill `[WEBSITE]`, test `/demo` on a phone, and make sure the Instagram profile has the
  launch post pinned and the demo link in the bio.
