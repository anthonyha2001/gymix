# "The Receipt": shareable reel #3

`gymix-receipt.mp4`: about 28 s, 1080×1920, with its own 110 BPM track (print-head chatter, a ka-ching, a paper tear).

## The idea
A receipt printer prints the **hidden monthly bill of running a gym without a system**, line by line on the beat:
"I'll pay next week" $135 · Expired, still training $90 · Wrong lira rate ×30 $48 · Notebook page 47 $45 · Cash that
"went somewhere" $60 · Chasing payments 9 h · "Kam el sa3er lyom?" 4 h · Checking who expired 3 h · Your weekend: gone.
**TOTAL $378 + 16 h** · "*** THANK YOU FOR NOT HAVING A SYSTEM ***"
Then: "That's **$4,536 a year**. Plus 192 hours of your life." The receipt is torn off and thrown away. "Now the
other receipt." Gymix prints: auto-frozen · set once · 1 screen · 1 tap · **TOTAL $29.99** · **CHANGE: your weekends
back** (ka-ching). End: "What does **your** gym's receipt say? Try the live demo."

## Why it drives clients (not just views)
- **Owners do the maths on their own gym while watching.** It sells the $29.99 by comparison, not by features.
- **The "receipt" format is unexpected** for software ads, and it's instantly readable with the sound off.
- **It invites a reply:** "what's on your receipt?" → comments → reach. Every comment is a warm lead to reply to with
  the demo link.
- Numbers are labelled on screen as an illustration (example gym, 80 members, $45/month), so nothing is overclaimed.
