# "Excuses gym owners hear every month": the shareable reel

`gymix-excuses.mp4`: 32.5 s, 1080×1920, with its own 128 BPM track. For Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels.

## Why it spreads
- **Everyone recognises it.** Members laugh ("that's literally me"), owners nod. Both share.
- **Tag bait:** "Which one is your favourite?" at the start, "Tag a gym owner who needs this" at the end.
- **Comment bait:** numbered excuses → people comment "#3 every day", "you forgot…".
- **Loopable:** the beat and the stamp rhythm make people rewatch, which the algorithm rewards.

## Launch playbook for maximum reach (no filming, almost no time)
1. **Post as a Reel** on Instagram and TikTok at the same time, Thursday or Sunday around 8–9 pm Beirut time.
2. **Cover image:** the first frame ("Excuses you hear every month."). Make sure it's selected as the cover.
3. **Add a trending sound at very low volume** (5–10%) on top of the built-in track: it gets the Reel into that
   sound's feed while the original beat stays audible.
4. **Reply to every comment in the first hour** (the agents can draft the replies). Early engagement decides reach.
5. **Share it to your Story** with a poll sticker: "Do you hear excuse #1?" Yes / Every day.
6. **Collab post:** invite 2–3 Lebanese fitness pages or coaches as Instagram "Collaborators" on the post (it then
   appears on their profiles too, with their followers). The Outreach agent can write the request.
7. **If it does well organically after 24 h, boost it** (same settings as the intro ad: Lebanon, 22–55, send to
   `[WEBSITE]/demo`). Boosting a post that already performs is the cheapest reach there is.
8. **Post a follow-up in 3–5 days:** "You told us the excuses we forgot" with the best comments (a carousel the team
   can make from the comments).

## Getting to the first 50 customers
Views alone don't make customers. The funnel:
Reel → profile (pinned launch post + "link in bio") → **live demo** (no sign-up) → **free trial** → paying.
- Put the demo link as the **first link in the bio**, with "Try a sample gym, no sign-up".
- Turn on an **Instagram auto-reply** for the keyword "DEMO": comment or DM "DEMO" and get the link (in the reel's
  pinned comment: "Comment DEMO and we'll send you the link").
- Every gym owner who starts a trial gets the nurture messages from the Onboarding agent.
- Measure weekly: views → profile visits → demo starts → trials → paying. The Analyst agent reports it.
