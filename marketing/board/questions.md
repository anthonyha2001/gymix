## Answered
- **Does the Shop appear in the demo's menu?** No: the shop is a lab feature, so it's not in the demo gym's menu
  (it joins when switched on for everyone). (Product, 9 Nov.)
- **Exact refusal wording on a tap (for videos):** a red screen, the reason, then "Please see the front desk."
  Reasons in the product: "Account frozen: unpaid balance of $X", "Membership is frozen", "Membership expired on
  <date>", "No membership", "No visits left", "Already checked in". Freezing Karim in the demo shows "Membership is
  frozen"; the unpaid wording needs a member with an app account who owes money, so for Reel 1 use the real wording
  as on-screen text or film it in a real trial gym with a staff member's own test account. (Product, 9 Nov.)
- **Is the tap simulator on in the demo gym?** Yes (Settings → Member app shows "Tap simulator: On"). In the member
  app on a computer, press Space twice. (Product, 9 Nov.)
- **The "sell a membership" button label:** "Renew / sell" on the member's profile. (Product, 9 Nov.)
- **What does a gym's NFC sticker open?** The gym's own check-in link (Settings → Check-in sticker), which checks the
  member in on their phone. A sample sticker for visits should be written with the demo or the trial link instead,
  not a real gym's check-in link. (Product, 9 Nov.)
- **Until WhatsApp is approved, how do gyms get members their login links?** On the member's profile, the invite
  card always has "Copy link" and "Copy full message", so the front desk can paste it anywhere. Once WhatsApp is
  connected, a "Send on WhatsApp" button sends it from Gymix. (Answered from the product, 9 Nov.)
- **What does the member's phone show when a tap is refused, and can it be filmed in the demo?** The tap screen
  shows that check-in was refused and why (expired, frozen, unpaid). In the demo, Karim's membership is active, so
  to film a refusal: as the owner, freeze Karim's membership on his profile, switch to the member app, and use the
  tap simulator (Space twice on a computer). Unfreeze him afterwards. (Answered from the product, 9 Nov.)
