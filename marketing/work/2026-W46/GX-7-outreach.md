# GX-7 · First messages, visit plan and follow-ups for the first 10 gyms (2026-W46)

## Read this first: when these messages may be sent

GX-1 could **not confirm that any of the 10 gyms is still open**, and **none of the 10 has a confirmed public business
contact** (the only row with one, Superhero Gym at The Smallville, isn't in the top 10). So:

- **Nothing in this file can go out cold today.** Each first message has a **"Send only if"** line. A message goes out
  only when **one** of these is true:
  1. **Public business contact.** The founder opened the gym's own Instagram or Facebook page (or its Google Maps card),
     saw a **recent post** (so it's open), and found a **business** account or number **published by the gym itself**.
     Use that account. Never a personal number, never a number from a third-party directory.
  2. **The owner reached out first** (a DM, a comment, a reply to a post).
  3. **You met the owner in person** and they agreed to hear from you. Then the message is a follow-up to the visit.
- **If a gym fails the open check** (no post in months, page gone), drop it and take a backup from GX-1.
- **You send every message yourself, one at a time, from your own account.** No bulk sending, no bought lists, no
  automation tools.
- **All Arabizi is marked NEEDS NATIVE CHECK.** Ask a Lebanese friend to read it before you send.
- **Not in any message:** automatic WhatsApp messages (reminders, receipts, login links), the demo's shop, door access,
  screens, occupancy, kiosk.

**The 2-minute open check (do it the evening before a visit or a message):** open the GX-1 `source` link. Is there a
post from the last few weeks? Is there an Instagram business account or a number in the gym's own bio? Note one real
detail from the latest post (a new class, a schedule, an event) for the `[DETAIL]` slot in the message. If you can't
find one, delete the slot: the messages work without it.

4. **Dollars and lira (25s).** Show a payment taken in lira at the gym's own rate. "Prices in dollars, take lira at

5. **Member app (20s).** Tap **"Member app"** to switch to the member's phone view (Karim's app): today's workout,
   membership and days left, classes. "Your members open a link, no App Store. They can add it to the home screen."
   Switch back.
6. **The ask (15s).** "I'll set it up with you now: 30 minutes, your plans, your first members, the sticker at the
   door. 14 days free, no card. $29.99 a month for the whole gym after that, no contract."

**Don't open:** the **shop** page (even if the guide makes it glow, skip it) and any chat or message log showing
automatic WhatsApp messages. If they ask about a turnstile or a gate: "Today it's the $1 sticker and a QR code, no
