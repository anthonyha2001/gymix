**Rules for this pack (from the card and brief 2.2, 2.5, 3, 7.2):**
- CTA on every piece: `[WEBSITE]/demo` (cold audience). Placeholders stay as written.
- No automatic WhatsApp reminders, receipts, welcome links or message log. Nothing in testing (no shop, screens,
  occupancy, kiosk, door access).
- Facts used, exactly as in the brief: an unpaid account **freezes automatically a set number of days after an
  invoice (3 by default, the owner chooses)**; **check-in is refused at the door**; **the next check-in after paying
  unfreezes it**; the front desk takes **cash, card, Whish or OMT**; **$29.99 a month for the whole gym**; **14 days
  free, no card**; the demo is a private sample gym, **no sign-up**, deleted after 24 hours.
- Abou Bkra and the Wasta Member are fictional types played by a friend who agreed. We tease the situation, never
  a person.
- Every caption is under 120 words (counted without hashtags), one joke, at most one emoji.
- Lebanese words are listed under each variant as **NEEDS NATIVE CHECK**.
- On-screen text: every line under 8 words. In-app screens (red refusal, green check-in) show whatever the app
  shows; our overlays never pretend to be app text. Per `board/questions.md`, the tap screen says check-in was
  refused and why (expired, frozen, unpaid).
