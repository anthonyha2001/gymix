## 4. Combined recording checklist

**Phone settings (every session)**
- Portrait 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps (60 for screen recordings if smooth), lock exposure/focus.
- Do Not Disturb on, notifications off, airplane mode off (the demo needs the internet), battery above 60%, brightness to max.
- Clean status bar: hide personal numbers and contacts; time can show. Close other apps. No personal photos, no real WhatsApp chats open.
- Wipe the lens, shoot with the rear camera, clean background with no real gym logo.
- Use the phone's built-in screen recorder for demo footage. Hide the recording indicator in the edit if needed.

**Session A: screen recording (about 30 min, in one sitting)**
Start a fresh demo right before; the sample gym is **deleted after 24 hours**. If you break the flow, press Exit and start a new demo.
1. `/demo` → "Start the demo" (Reel 7 opening). Re-do this take if the loader is slow.
2. The "Welcome to Atlas Fitness" guide card and the "Demo gym" bar (Reel 7 2-5 s). Don't press "Start the tour" unless you want it.
3. Dashboard → Check-in → "Rami" → check in. Then "Tarek Mansour" → refused (Reel 7).
4. A member profile → sell / renew a membership (Reel 7). [TO CONFIRM exact label.]
5. **Member app** → Karim's phone: home, "Day A: Push", "Start workout", weekly days, membership card (Reel 7). Stay on this page for a second, steady.
6. Back to staff side. Open Karim's profile and **freeze** his membership. Press **Member app** and double-tap Space (green first if you want a "before"). Capture the **red** screen. (Needed for Reel 1 and Abou Bkra.)
7. Back to staff, **unfreeze** Karim, member app, double-tap Space. Capture the **green** screen "Checked in / Welcome back, Karim".
8. Capture each screen 3 times: 3 s each.
