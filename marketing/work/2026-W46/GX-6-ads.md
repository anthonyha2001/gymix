## 5. Stop and scale rules (rules, not predicted numbers)

`N` values are for the founder to set before launch, based on what `[AD_BUDGET]` buys. I do not know costs here and will not guess them. Suggested method: set `N` so that stopping an ad costs no more than a fixed share of its budget.

**Day 3 (health check, no judging yet)**
- If an ad has not been approved or delivered anything, fix the ad (rejection reason, payment method, audience too small), do not change the creative.
- If an ad set spends and shows `[N1]` or more link clicks to `/demo` but **zero demos started**, check the page first (does `/demo` load on mobile? does the button work? is the pixel firing?) before blaming the ad.
- If any placement or ad shows policy trouble or hostile comments, pause that ad and ask Guardian.
- Do not pause for low results yet. Too few clicks says nothing.

**Day 7 (first decision)**
- **Pause** any ad with `[N2]` or more link clicks and **no demo started**.
- **Pause** an ad whose cost per demo started is clearly the highest of the five and above the founder's tolerance (the founder sets the tolerance; no number from me).
- **Keep** ads that have produced demos started; do not touch them.
- **Move budget:** shift the budget share from paused ads to the ad with the lowest cost per demo started, keeping ad set 2 (studios) at 30% unless it has produced no demos at all, in which case pause it and move that 30% to ad set 1.
- Check placements: if one placement spends a large share and yields no demos, remove it.
- **Test next:** one new variant built from the best ad's hook, not a new idea. (For example, if A1 wins, a second Abou Bkra ep. from GX-3.)

**Day 14 (end of test)**
- **Scale** only an ad that produced demos started **and** at least one trial started. Raise its budget gradually (no more than about 20% at a time, every 2 to 3 days) rather than doubling.
- **Stop** the whole campaign if there are zero trials and the cost per demo is above the founder's tolerance. Write down why (creative, audience, page, or too little data).
- **Hold and re-test** if demos came but no trials: the problem is the demo-to-trial step, not the ad. Report it to the founder; do not buy more clicks to hide it.
- Always read demo to trial conversion and trials started alongside spend (brief 8.4). Cost per click alone is not a result.
- No claims of results in any public post until real numbers exist.
