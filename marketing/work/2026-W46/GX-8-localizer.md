## Phrases I'm least sure about (for the native check)

1. **"Kassro. Mano gym-ak. Lessa."** (Reel 7 hook): is "lessa" alone, at the end, read as "not yet"? Alternative:
   "Kassro. Ba3do mesh gym-ak." Also "gym" is treated as masculine (kassro, gym-ak); check that's what people say.
2. **"Kel wa7ad bibi3ak software bi2ellak 'sahle ktir, wallah'"**: does it tease the situation and not sound like an
   attack on salespeople in general?
3. **"Dir gym la yom."** (Reel 7 end card, "Run a gym for a day"): "dir" may sound like "spin"; alternatives:
   "Sir owner la yom" (be an owner for a day) or "Shaghghel gym la yom".
4. **"Kabse wa7de"** (one button / one press): natural, or would people say "click wa7ad"?
5. **"Ma 3ad fi 7arj 3al bab."** (Reel 1): does "7arj" (embarrassment) read right, or is "bala 7aki bi2a33ed" more
   natural?
6. **"w el tap el jeye bifawwto"** (Reel 1, Ep. 1 "el bab ma ba2a yfawwto"): verb forms and spelling.
7. **"3 bel default, enta bt2arrer"**: natural, or "3 iyyem 3adatan, w enta bt8ayyer"?
8. **"Huwwe 3an jadd m2amen bi bkra. W m2amen fiya kel yom"** (Ep. 1): grammar of "fiya" (bkra as feminine?) and
   whether "m2amen" sounds too religious. If so: "Huwwe 3an jadd metfa2el bi bkra."
9. **"Btrayye7 el a3sab"** (Ep. 1, "it's therapeutic"): funny enough, or flat?
10. **"Hayda sport tene"** (Ep. 2): natural Lebanese, or "hayda shi tene"?
11. **"min ma kent ta3ref"** (Ep. 2, "whoever you know").
12. **"W akhiran, eja bkra."** (Ep. 3 hook): "akhiran" may sound formal; alternative "W eja bkra, akhiran." or
    "Bkra, wassal."
13. **"El front desk sa22af"** (Ep. 3, the desk clapped) and **"yzakkro bi lotf"** (remind him nicely): natural, or
    keep "remind him nicely" in English as a quote?
14. **Gender:** captions say "enta" (masc.) to the owner, as most Lebanese posts do. Fine for Maya-type studio
    owners too, or should the studio-facing pieces switch to neutral phrasing?
15. **French "tu" vs "vous"**: I used "tu" (Instagram, the friendly voice). Check it isn't too familiar for older
    French-speaking owners.
16. **French "salle" vs "gym"**: Lebanese French speakers often say "le gym". "Salle" is correct French; check which
    sounds more local. Also "dollars et livres" for dollars and lira (or "dollars et LL").
17. **French "Nouveau tap."** (Reel 1, line 3): clear, or "Il refait un tap."?
18. **Written prices**: kept `$29.99` in French too so the number is identical everywhere; a French proofreader may
    want "19,99 $". Founder's call.
