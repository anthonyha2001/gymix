# Gymix marketing plan: week 2026-W46 (Mon 9 to Sun 15 November 2026)

**Week 1 of the launch.** Area: **Greater Beirut and Metn only** (brief 4.2). No previous week, no Analyst report, an
empty board. So this week's plan is about getting the first gym owners into the live demo and the first trials started
without wasting the founder's hours.

**Ground rules for this week (founder's notes + the brief):**
- **Cold CTA everywhere: the live demo `[WEBSITE]/demo`** (no sign-up). Warm CTA: `[WEBSITE]/signup` or
  `[WHATSAPP_SALES]`. Keep every placeholder exactly as written.
- **No content that advertises automatic WhatsApp messages** (reminders, receipts, welcome links, the message log).
  They aren't confirmed connected and approved by Meta (brief 2.2, questions.md). This touches the stock assets too:
  the one-pager (9.3, "WhatsApp renewal reminders"), the 3 steps (9.2, step 2), the Google ad descriptions (9.6) and
  nurture day 4 (9.8). Leave those lines out or rewrite them.
- **No paid ads go live this week.** A plan is fine; `[AD_BUDGET]` stays a placeholder.
- **Nothing "in testing" on screen or in copy:** door access, shop/POS, screens, live occupancy, kiosk. The demo has a
  **shop** page. **Don't record it or show it** in Reel 7 or anywhere else.
- No testimonials, gym counts, customer logos or discounts. There are no customers yet.
- **AI budget: 10 cards** (8 drafting, 2 Guardian checks).
