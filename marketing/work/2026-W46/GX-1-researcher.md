# GX-1 · Lead list: 40 gyms and studios in Beirut and Metn (2026-W46)

**Read this first (honesty note).** Every row comes from a public listing found by web search (Instagram, Facebook, Beirut.com, Onlife Lebanon, CrossFit affiliate pages, directories). I could not open Instagram or Google Maps from here, so I could **not** check recent posts or reviews. "Still operating" is therefore **not confirmed** for any row. Before each visit, open the Instagram or Facebook link and check the last post is recent (a 2-minute job per gym). Anything marked (?) is extra uncertain. Sizes are guesses. The `maps` column is a Google Maps search link (not a verified pin). Phone numbers appear in directories for many gyms, but I only put a `public_contact` where the gym or its host page itself publishes it (one row). For the rest, take the number from the gym's own Instagram bio or Google Maps card.

Areas the search did not cover well: Hamra, Dbayeh, Sin el Fil, Baabda (almost nothing independent came up; Sin el Fil only via a PT studio tagged to it). I also found no confirmed women-only gym inside Metn this week (the women-only ones found are in Ras el Nabeh, Haret Hreik, Tayouneh and Jnah, i.e. Beirut or the southern suburbs). Ask the founder if he wants those added next week.
